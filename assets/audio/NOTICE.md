# Dutch audio credits

These MP3 files were synthesized locally from this project's original Dutch learning text using Piper 1.8.0 and the Dutch Netherlands Ronnie and Pim medium models. The generated clips are distributed under the project's MIT license.

- Piper engine: https://github.com/OHF-Voice/piper1-gpl (GPL-3.0; optional authoring tool, not bundled with the site)
- Model source: https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices
- Pinned revision: 6b9cfaacb4fb2e02a9621227e3c1a48bec9710c3
- Models: nl_NL-ronnie-medium, nl_NL-pim-medium
- Model cards: the training and base datasets are identified as CC0. Copies follow in `ronnie.MODEL_CARD` and `pim.MODEL_CARD`.

No model weights or Piper runtime are included in this directory. No private conversations or account data were used to generate the clips.
