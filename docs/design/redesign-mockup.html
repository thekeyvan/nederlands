Mijn Nederlands, maar dan speels.

A redesign proposal for nederlands.keyvanmahmoudi.com, built from shadcn/ui components themed in Dutch orange and navy. The content, the SM-2 engine and the Dutch interface stay. Practice becomes active, every answer gets feedback, and the progress you really made shows up as streaks, XP, stars and stamps. Switch your system to dark mode to see the dark theme.

What I found on the live site

I reviewed it at 1280px and 375px, did a full practice round, and read the content model. The content is strong: 624 words, 105 sentences, 20 dialogues, 48 grammar patterns and real audio. What feels flat is the experience around it.

Now

Practice is passive

Every card is "think, reveal, rate yourself 1–4". You never actually answer and hear that you're right.

Proposal

Five game modes mixed into each round: choose, listen, type, match pairs and the classic card. The answer still feeds SM-2.

Now

No reward moments

A right answer just shows the next card. The end screen is a grey circle and "5 kaartjes geoefend".

Proposal

Green and red answer states, a +XP pop, a combo counter, a cheering mascot, confetti and newly earned stamps.

Now

Home is a long read

Three competing calls to action, then two paragraphs of course instructions and an 8-item accordion before you reach the themes.

Proposal

One big "Dagmissie" button. The course becomes a visual route, and themes become illustrated cards with stars.

Now

Progress doesn't pull you back

Thin bars and a 7-day table. Nothing gives you a reason to come back tomorrow.

Proposal

A daily streak, XP with playful levels, 0–3 stars per theme and a stamp card, all calculated from real review history.

Now

Mobile friction

After you reveal an answer, the rating buttons fall below the fold. The top bar is crowded, and the filter chips wrap onto two lines.

Proposal

Full-screen practice with answers in the thumb zone, a bottom Drawer for feedback, a slim top bar and chips that scroll sideways.

Now

Reads like a magazine

Muted beige, hairline borders and serif italics feel calm and editorial, not playful.

Proposal

shadcn/ui as the system, with bolder orange and navy, a pressable "game" button variant, illustrations and a mascot.

The game layer is calculated from real practice

Every review is already saved with a time and a grade, so none of these numbers are made up. There are no hearts or lives: a wrong answer never locks you out.

Reeks

Consecutive days with at least one card practised, counted in the learner's timezone. Week dots show which days.

XP & niveaus

+10 for a correct answer, +5 for an attempt and +2 per combo step. Toerist → Nieuwkomer → Buurtbewoner → Fietser → Collega → Kletskous → Grachtenkenner → Polderkampioen. The level names are for fun and are not CEFR levels.

Dagmissie

A daily goal ring you set to 5, 10 or 20 cards, plus two small daily quests such as "Luister naar 5 zinnen".

De route

The 8 course steps become stops on a canal map. The mascot stands where you are, and a stop turns green once you've practised its cards.

Stempelkaart

Collectible stamps, like a Dutch loyalty card: a 7-day streak, a first 3-star theme, 100 words, the full route.

Sterren per thema

One star when every card is practised, two at 50% well remembered, three at 90%. Uses the existing progressStatus().

Laptop: Vandaag

Three columns: a shadcn sidebar on the left, one mission plus the route and themes in the middle, and your streak, level, quests and stamps on the right, always in view.

shadcn/ui theme and components

Standard shadcn CSS variables with a Dutch palette and a larger radius (1rem) for a friendlier feel. Two custom additions: a "game" Button variant with a pressable bottom edge, and success / xp / info tokens for game feedback. Icons stay Lucide, the set the site already ships. The illustrations were generated with Codex in one shared palette.

--secondaryNavy

--successGoed

--infoLuisteren

--accentActief

--backgroundRoom

Build note: shadcn/ui is React + Tailwind. The site today is plain HTML, CSS and JS. Implementing this means moving the app to Vite + React + Tailwind. The content files, locales, audio, SM-2 logic and Supabase sync carry over unchanged.
